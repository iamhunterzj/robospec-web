Architecture Perspective

Teleoperation is not a product we sell.
It is part of the HERO learning infrastructure.

We do not treat teleoperation as a permanent manual crutch or a detached hardware product. Instead, it serves as the essential real-world sensory bridge that captures expert human dexterity and funnels high-value ground-truth training signals directly into HERO’s continuous learning loop.

Zero Stoppage Autonomy

Human-in-the-Loop by Design

ROBOSPEC is built for practical deployment, not all-or-nothing autonomy that halts operations when edge cases occur. When robots encounter low-confidence states, humans supervise, intervene, and correct in real time.
