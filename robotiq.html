Authorized Agency Overview

RoboSpec is proud to serve as an authorized distributor and solutions integrator for Robotiq in Singapore. Robotiq is the global benchmark for collaborative robot end-effectors, known for unmatched adaptability, rugged industrial durability, and seamless plug-and-play integration with cobots and Physical AI platforms.

Adaptive 2-Finger Grippers (2F-85 / 2F-140)

  • Flexible Grasping Modes: Parallel, encompassing, inside pick, and wide grasp modes adapt automatically to varying part geometries.
  • Precise Grip Control: Software-controllable position, speed, and force (up to 235 N on 2F-85; up to 125 N on 2F-140).
  • Built-in Part Detection: Real-time grip validation feedback to confirm secure workpiece acquisition.

Hand-E Precision Electric Gripper

  • High-Accuracy Assembly: 50 mm stroke with sub-millimeter precision, tailored for electronics, lab automation, and tight tolerances.
  • IP67 Rated: Waterproof, dustproof, and resistant to cutting fluids for harsh CNC machine tending environments.

Vacuum Grippers & FT 300-S Force Sensors

  • AirPick & EPick: All-electric and venturi vacuum generators engineered for palletizing, cardboard carton handling, and flat component picking without noisy external airlines.
  • FT 300-S 6-Axis Force Torque Sensor: Digital signal processing enabling constant force application for precision insertion, sanding, deburring, and polishing.
Local Inventory, Warranty & Integration Services

As Robotiq’s official partner in Singapore, RoboSpec provides rapid local delivery, official manufacturer warranty, mounting flange adapters, ROS/Python drivers, and custom integration with Franka FR3, Mobile FR3 Duo, and Diana 7 robot arms.
