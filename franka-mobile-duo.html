Platform Overview

The Franka Mobile FR3 Duo combines two high-sensitivity Franka Research 3 arms with Franka’s Tactile Mobile Robot (TMR) base into a unified, research-ready embodiment. Built from the ground up for embodied AI and physical manipulation research, it eliminates the boundaries between stationary robotic workcells and dynamic real-world environments.

Dual-Arm Bimanual Kinematics

  • Kinematic Reach: Dual 7-DOF arms with redundant kinematic flexibility matching human ergonomics.
  • Torque Sensitivity: High-bandwidth torque sensors on all 14 joints for compliant, safe interaction and contact-rich manipulation.
  • Payload Capacity: 3 kg payload per arm (6 kg total dual-arm capacity).

Omnidirectional Mobility & Embedded Compute

  • Tactile Mobile Robot (TMR) Base: Holonomic omnidirectional mobility for tight lab and industrial corridor navigation.
  • Onboard NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin: Powers real-time VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models, spatial SLAM, and low-latency motor control.
  • Multimodal Sensor Array: Dual 2D/3D safety LiDARs, IMU, dual wrist-mounted cameras (Intel RealSense), and head-mounted stereo camera bracket.

AI Training & Imitation Learning Workflows

  • Franka GELLO Duo Compatible: Direct joint-to-joint dual-arm teleoperation for intuitive human demonstration recording and dataset generation.
  • Simulation & Digital Twins: Official physics-accurate models in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, MuJoCo, and ROS 2.
  • Franka Control Interface (FCI): Unrestricted 1 kHz torque, joint velocity, and Cartesian impedance control.
